Does Veilwise change my settings automatically?

No. For your security, no app can change another app's or the system's privacy settings. Veilwise explains each step in plain language and points you to the exact place in your iPhone's Settings, so you stay in control.

What's the difference between free and Premium?

Eight essential steps are free forever. Premium unlocks 12 advanced protections plus future additions. You can choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Veilwise, go to Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases. Your Premium access is tied to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Veilwise and choose Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the current period ends. (Lifetime purchases never expire and don't need cancelling.)

Do you collect any of my data?

No. Veilwise has no account, no analytics, and no servers. Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Which iPhones are supported?

Veilwise runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
